=========================================
          SIX PILLARS OF SUCCESS
=========================================
[ Before you chase success, know the 6 things it will take from you. ]

1. PATIENCE
Success is slow, not silent – life tests those who want more than the average.
While others rush, you wait, build, and grow.

Patience doesn't mean doing nothing — it means trusting the process while working.
The seeds you water today will become the life you live tomorrow.
If you're losing patience, then you are losing the battle.

2. HARD WORK
Dreams don't respond to wishes — they respond to effort.
Everyone wants the result, but very few want the grind behind it.

Hard work makes you sharper, stronger, and impossible to replace.
Give more than expected — and the world gives back more than imagined.
If you want the throne, earn the scars.

3. NEVER GIVE UP
Every champion has a story filled with failures — the difference is they didn't stop.
When life pushes you down, stand again — even if you're shaking.

Winners aren't the smartest — they're the ones who refuse to quit.
Keep going even when motivation dies — consistency will carry you.
Fall down 1000 times. Stand up 1001 times.

4. DISCIPLINE
Discipline is doing what needs to be done — even when you don't feel like it.
Motivation is a spark; discipline is the fire that keeps burning.

Your habits build your destiny, and your discipline protects it.
Say 'no' more often — especially to the things that distract you.
Discipline turns potential into power.

5. SELF - CONFIDENCE
Believe in yourself even when no one else does — especially then.
Confidence isn't loud — it's the silent understanding that you can and you will.

When you trust your abilities, the world adjusts to match that belief.
Walk like you already earned what you're working toward.
The strongest foundation you'll ever build is belief in yourself.

6. SACRIFICE
Success asks a price — comfort, sleep, excuses, and sometimes people.
You can't become someone new while holding on to the habits of the old you.

Sacrifice now or regret later — the choice is yours.
Trade weekends for work, distractions for focus, pleasure for purpose.
If you don't sacrifice for what you want, then what you want becomes the sacrifice.
